# LidRipple 1.0.0

Free, open-source Fold and Ripple effects for your MacBook desktop.

## Install

1. Download **lidripple-1.0.0.dmg** below (not the source-code archive).
2. Open it and drag **LidRipple** into **Applications**.
3. Launch LidRipple, grant Screen Recording permission, and quit/reopen if prompted.
4. Choose Fold or Ripple from the menu-bar icon. Preview Effect works without capture permission.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later and a built-in display. The app is universal
(Apple Silicon and Intel), Developer ID signed, notarized, and stapled.
Physical lid behavior was owner-tested on M4/M2 MacBooks; this is not a claim that
all MacBook models have been qualified.

## Know before downloading

- Lid-following effects require an available lid-angle sensor.
- Without a sensor, fallback is experimental/unverified on real no-HID hardware:
  no closing animation; a timed opening reveal may run after unlock.
- Effects cannot appear over the lock screen or before macOS makes the desktop
  available. A brief system-desktop glimpse can still occur during wake.
- If macOS cannot confirm an unlocked session, open LidRipple's menu after unlock
  to resume effects. Protected video may appear black in the effect.
- Captured screen pixels stay temporarily on-device; the app does not save or
  upload them. Preferences and pixel-free diagnostic logs may be stored locally.
- DMG installation only for v1; Homebrew support is deferred.
- Not affiliated with Apple; no pixel-identical reference-animation claim.
